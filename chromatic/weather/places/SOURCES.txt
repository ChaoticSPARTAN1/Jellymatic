ZIP Code Tabulation Areas approximate USPS ZIP codes; a few ZIP codes (post-office boxes, single
buildings) have none. Looking a place up sends nothing anywhere; only the chosen point is then sent
to api.weather.gov.

us-town-sizes.txt.gz: each place's land area, which the location pack maker (scripts/make_location_pack.py)
uses to choose the larger towns near a place (the bundled tables hold no population).

Made by chromatic/weather/tools/make_town_sizes.py from the same 2024 Gazetteer place file as us-places.txt.gz
(2024_Gaz_place_national.zip above, same SHA-256; public domain). Kept: 14,895 places of 2 square miles of
land or more (6,339 of them census-designated places, which the maker counts at a third): state, name, internal
point, land area in square miles, kind.
