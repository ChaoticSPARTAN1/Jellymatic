# Media

Trailers, short clips and pictures for Jellymatic and its TV and ChromaTube apps.

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `jellymatic/` | Jellymatic trailer (1080x1080 and 1920x1080), four short clips (start-up menu, Jellyfin, Chromatic Manager, Remote Pilot), teaser pictures |
| `howto/` | How to stream Jellyfin over Wi-Fi from a Chromatic to an Analogue Pocket with a link cable (42 s, both sizes), and a picture. See [Pocket link](../docs/Pocket-link.md) |
| `apps/` | Promos for the start-up menu's TV row (Chromavision: Local Weather and Live TV) and ChromaTube, both sizes, and a still picture of each |

All videos are MP4 (H.264, 30 fps, AAC sound). Stream quality on the consoles may vary.

The apps' promos are also in the
[Chromavision](https://github.com/ChaoticSPARTAN1/Chromavision/tree/main/media) and
[ChromaTube](https://github.com/ChaoticSPARTAN1/ChromaTube/tree/main/promo) repositories. The PocketFin
trailer and clips are in the [PocketFin](https://github.com/ChaoticSPARTAN1/PocketFin/tree/main/media)
repository.
